The gap

Where new business slips away

Most businesses don't lose customers because of their product. They lose them in the space between someone reaching out and someone getting back to them.

Slow replies

A new inquiry sits in an inbox while the person asking contacts the next business on their list.

After-hours inquiries

Evenings and weekends are when many people finally have time to reach out, and when no one is there to answer.

No follow-up

Interested people go quiet after the first reply, and a busy team doesn't have time to check back in.

No-shows

Booked appointments get forgotten without a timely reminder, leaving empty slots on the calendar.

What our agents do

Custom agents for the tasks that cause the gap

Each agent is built for one business and its way of working. It handles the repetitive steps so your people can spend their time on the conversations that matter.

Instant replies to new inquiries

A prompt, friendly first response, day or night, so a new lead hears back from you first.

Follow-up

Polite check-ins on a schedule you set, so interested people don't drift away.

Scheduling

Offers open times and books calls or appointments directly on your calendar.

Reminders

Timely confirmations and reminders before appointments to help cut down on no-shows.

Answers to common questions

Hours, services, what to bring, next steps, all in wording you've reviewed and approved.

Urgent items to a human

Anything sensitive, complex or time-critical is flagged and handed to the right person on your team.
